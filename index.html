The Wheeled-Leg Advantage

ARES-Bot leverages the T-REX wheeled-leg robot platform. This hybrid design integrates the efficiency of wheel-drive systems for rapid movement across flat surfaces (like concrete and asphalt) with the terrain adaptability of legged mechanisms for complex, uneven environments.

  • High Energy Efficiency

    Faster speeds and lower energy consumption on flat roads compared to traditional legged bots.

  • Terrain Adaptability

    PID/LQR controlled leg extension and retraction allows body levelling and obstacle crossing.

  • Edge Computing Power

    Powered by the Horizon RDK-X3 platform, executing complex neural networks directly on the edge.

Core Capabilities

A fully autonomous suite designed for patrol, detection, and rescue operations.

SLAM Navigation

LiDAR and Visual Inertial Odometry (VIO) fusion for robust indoor and outdoor mapping, waypoint patrol, and obstacle avoidance.

AI Vision System

Real-time object detection using YOLO and DeepSORT running on TensorRT. Capable of human tracking and suspicious activity recognition.

Victim Localization

Fusion of thermal imaging and audio microphone arrays to detect heat signatures and localize shouting in search-and-rescue scenarios.

Wheeled-Leg Balancing

Advanced LQR control systems for self-balancing, dual-leg length control for roll compensation, and scissor-effect mitigation.

ROS2 Framework

Modular software architecture built on ROS Noetic / ROS2 Humble. Distributed nodes for control, perception, and application layers.

Remote Dashboard

Web-based remote monitoring interface displaying robot maps, streaming multi-modal camera feeds, and generating AI-driven alerts.

System Architecture

A multi-layered approach enabling robust sensor fusion and edge AI processing.
